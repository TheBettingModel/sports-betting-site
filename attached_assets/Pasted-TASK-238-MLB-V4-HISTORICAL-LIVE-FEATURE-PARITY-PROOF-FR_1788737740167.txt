TASK #238 — MLB V4 HISTORICAL/LIVE FEATURE-PARITY PROOF & FROZEN INPUT CONTRACT

PROJECT: The Betting Model
DATE: September 6, 2026

MISSION

Prove, with deterministic evidence, that the historical MLB V4 research representation and the live MLB V4 model-input pipeline mean the same thing.

This task exists because Task #237 identified a hard methodological blocker:

A research candidate exists and shows some signal, but the historical 38-feature research schema does not yet have proven semantic parity with the live nested `model-input-v4` contract.

1. historically, using only legitimate point-in-time evidence, and
