============================================================
MISSION
============================================================

#223 is the formal evidence gate between:

"we built a legitimate sports model"

and

"this model is safe and justified to become the production NCAAF
champion."

The purpose is to determine:

1. Whether historical out-of-sample performance is stable enough.
2. Whether V4 probabilities are adequately calibrated.
3. Whether the score/margin/total distribution behaves realistically.
4. Whether the 2026 prospective predictions can be legitimately
evaluated.
5. Whether V4 should advance toward controlled production approval.
6. Whether any calibration transform is actually justified.
7. Whether Baseline D remains the correct frozen production candidate.

This is NOT yet the public go-live task.
