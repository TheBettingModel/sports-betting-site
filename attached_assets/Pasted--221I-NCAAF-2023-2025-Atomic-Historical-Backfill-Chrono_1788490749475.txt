CORE PRINCIPLE
============================================================

We do NOT require TBM to have physically captured a database snapshot in 2024 in order for every historical feature to be PIT-safe.

A feature may legitimately be reconstructed if it can be deterministically derived from atomic events that occurred BEFORE the historical target game's kickoff.

Example:

For a 2024 Week 8 game:

VALID:
derive season-to-date offense using completed Weeks 1–7.

INVALID:
use final 2024 season offense.

VALID:
calculate pregame Elo by sequentially updating ratings after Weeks 1–7.

INVALID:
use an Elo/rating calculated using Week 8 or future results.

VALID:
derive QB historical performance from prior games.

INVALID:
use target-game QB statistics.

The historical replay engine must behave as though it is moving through history one game at a time.

============================================================
