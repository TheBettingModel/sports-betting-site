#219 — TBM Recommendation / Publication Status Audit & Separation

We need to stop and cleanly audit a confusing behavior before doing any further sports-model development.

DO NOT begin NCAAF V4.
DO NOT begin NFL V4.
DO NOT modify MLB V3/V4/V4.1.
DO NOT change any sports model formulas.
DO NOT change model weights.
DO NOT change model thresholds.
DO NOT change edge calculations.
DO NOT change confidence calculations.
DO NOT change units.
DO NOT change approval requirements.
DO NOT weaken evidence gates.
DO NOT change publication eligibility.
DO NOT change grading.
DO NOT change Results or ROI.
DO NOT change Free Pick, Top Pick, Play of the Day, or notifications.

This task is about understanding and correctly representing the difference between:

1. WHAT THE SPORTS MODEL ACTUALLY RECOMMENDS

and

2. WHETHER THAT RECOMMENDATION IS ALLOWED TO BE PUBLISHED.

==================================================
CURRENT ISSUE
==================================================

The dashboard is currently showing approximately 20 picks as "Neutral."

A preliminary investigation found:

- #214B MLB research collection did NOT change pick calculations.
- The slate included NCAAF, MLB, and Soccer.
- The publication-safety layer can convert the displayed recommendation to Neutral when the exact model/market approval is missing or is not PRODUCTION_APPROVED.
- NCAAF also has an evidence gate that can prevent publication.
- Therefore some rows displayed as Neutral may NOT actually be model-generated Neutral recommendations.
- They may be stronger underlying model recommendations that were intentionally blocked from publication.

This makes the Admin dashboard misleading.

We need to audit this carefully and separate these concepts permanently.

==================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Create a clean separation between:

RAW MODEL OPINION

PUBLICATION DECISION

PUBLIC DISPLAY

The desired conceptual structure is:

raw_model_recommendation
        ↓
publication evaluation
        ↓
publication_status
        ↓
publication_block_reason
        ↓
public_display_recommendation

Example:

raw_model_recommendation = "Strong Buy"

publication_status = "BLOCKED"

publication_block_reason = "MODEL_NOT_PRODUCTION_APPROVED"

public_display_recommendation = "Neutral"

This is fundamentally different from:

raw_model_recommendation = "Neutral"

publication_status = "NOT_APPLICABLE_NO_PLAY"

publication_block_reason = null

public_display_recommendation = "Neutral"

Admin must be able to distinguish these cases.

Public/subscriber safety must remain unchanged.
