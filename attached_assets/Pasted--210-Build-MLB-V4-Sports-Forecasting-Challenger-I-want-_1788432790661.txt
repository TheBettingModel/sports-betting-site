==================================================
PROBLEM WITH CURRENT MLB MODEL
==================================================

The forensic audit found the production MLB model currently relies heavily on:

- Record
- Pythagorean strength
- Last-five form
- Run differential
- 1.3 confidence multiplier

Current production weights are approximately:

record:              0.396624
Pythagorean:         0.579707
form:                0.235502
score differential: 0.083171
rest:                0.002
confidence:          1.30

These materially exceed the original priors.

At the same time:

starting pitcher is capped at ±8 percentage points
bullpen workload is capped at ±4
lineup is capped at ±6

and lineup contributes zero when unconfirmed.

There is also no aggregate cap on the team-level factors.

The result is that historical/team-level strength can overwhelm today's individual matchup.

The current model also adds deterministic game-ID hash noise of approximately:

-3 pp to +4 pp

even though this is not sports evidence.

The global calibration rule is also a fixed piecewise adjustment rather than empirical MLB calibration.

MLB V4 should be designed specifically to correct these structural weaknesses.
