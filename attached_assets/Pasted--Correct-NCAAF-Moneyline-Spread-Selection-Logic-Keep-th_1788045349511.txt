# Correct NCAAF Moneyline + Spread Selection Logic

Keep the existing NCAAF moneyline model as the production baseline while the NCAAF spread model remains in shadow validation.

However, do not design the permanent system as “moneyline first and spread only if moneyline fails.”

For every eligible NCAAF game, always independently generate:

1. Moneyline Candidate
2. Spread Candidate

The moneyline candidate must retain its existing probability, fair price, no-vig probability, edge, expected value, confidence, recommendation, units, safeguards, and historical behavior.

The spread candidate must independently contain:

* Selected team
* Exact sportsbook spread
* Exact sportsbook price
* Opposing line and price
* Model fair spread
* Model cover probability at the exact line
* No-vig market cover probability
* Model edge
* Expected value
* Push probability where applicable
* Prediction uncertainty
* Data-quality score
* Confidence
* Recommendation
* Units
* Sportsbook
* Timestamp
* Opening line
* Current line
* Closing line once available
* Spread CLV
* Model version
* Shadow/production status

During shadow mode:

* Generate the spread candidate for every supported game.
* Permanently store the prediction.
* Grade it after the game.
* Track Brier score, Brier Skill Score, log loss, calibration, ROI, units, CLV, drawdown, ATS performance, and segmented performance.
* Do not allow the spread to replace the official moneyline selection.
* Do not include shadow spread results in official published records.
* Do not alter existing moneyline recommendations or historical predictions.
* Preserve all existing NCAAF Neutral/zero-unit protections.

The internal analytics/admin system must show the spread candidate even while it is in shadow mode so we can determine what the spread model predicted and how it performed.

Do not hide whether the spread was evaluated.

Display internally:

Moneyline Candidate
vs.
Spread Candidate
vs.
Official Selected Market

If the spread model has not been production-approved, the official selection remains the existing qualified moneyline candidate.

Once the NCAAF spread model passes its independent validation gates, remove automatic moneyline priority.

At that point the workflow becomes:

Moneyline Candidate
+
Spread Candidate
→ Independently validate both
→ Compare calibrated expected value, probability quality, uncertainty, price quality, CLV history, market quality, data quality, and risk
→ Select the strongest risk-adjusted market
→ Publish one primary official market per game

Therefore, once both markets are validated, a qualifying moneyline must NOT automatically prevent a superior spread from becoming the official selection.

Example:

Moneyline:
Model probability 58%
EV +3.2%
Confidence 72

Spread:
Team -2.5 -110
Cover probability 56.8%
EV +8.4%
Confidence 77

If both models are production-approved and the spread has the stronger validated risk-adjusted value, the spread should become the official selection.

The system should not favor moneyline simply because it was evaluated first.

The permanent objective is:

**Choose the best validated betting market for the game, not the first qualifying betting market.**

Until spread validation is complete:

**Moneyline = Production**

**Spread = Shadow Challenger**

After spread validation:

**Moneyline vs Spread = Market Competition**

and the strongest validated risk-adjusted candidate becomes the official play.
