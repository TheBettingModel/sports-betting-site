7. PIT STANDARD
==================================================

For event G:

every feature used to forecast G must be constructible using information available before G's prediction cutoff.

Rolling historical statistics may use:

events completed before G.

They may not use:

G's outcome
future games
end-of-season aggregates containing G/future games
postgame state
retrospective lineup/starter knowledge not known pregame

==================================================
8. SAFE HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION
==================================================

It is legitimate to reconstruct pregame rolling statistics from historical completed games when the calculation is strictly chronological.

Example:

Before NBA game 50:

team rolling offensive state may be calculated from completed games 1-49.

It may NOT use:

game 50 outcome
games 51+
final season average

This distinction should allow much richer historical training without requiring literal archived snapshots for every basic rolling statistic.
