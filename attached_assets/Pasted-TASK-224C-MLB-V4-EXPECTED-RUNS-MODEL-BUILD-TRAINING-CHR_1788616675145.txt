SECTION 26 — FAIR MONEYLINE
==================================================

Convert frozen model probability to fair American odds.

For p > 0.5:

fair_price = -100 * p / (1-p)

For p < 0.5:

fair_price = 100 * (1-p) / p

Handle p = 0.5 as +100.

Persist sufficient precision internally.

Do not round before downstream calculations.
