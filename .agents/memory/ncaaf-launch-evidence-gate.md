---
name: NCAAF launch evidence gate
description: Why early-season college-football forecasts remain no-bet until independent evidence is available.
---

NCAAF recommendations must remain Neutral with zero units until the independent challenger has passed a separate promotion decision. Schedules, logos, sportsbook prices, and forecast snapshots should still flow normally.

**Why:** Opening-week records and team-stat samples can be empty, leaving the generic fallback driven mostly by home advantage and deterministic noise. That can manufacture a large apparent edge with no team-specific basis. Market odds may be displayed but cannot stand in for an independent prediction. A zero-unit challenger must also bypass published-pick and pick-result creation, because generic publication code may normalize nonpositive stakes back to a real wager and contaminate ROI, grading, and learning.

**How to apply:** Keep the conservative gate in every live, scheduled, and replay path. Store challenger forecasts only as immutable prediction/feature evidence; never publish, grade, or train from them before promotion. Replace or relax the gate only after point-in-time walk-forward validation, and preserve the evidence/no-bet reason in immutable snapshots.
