---
name: NCAAF launch evidence gate
description: Why early-season college-football forecasts remain no-bet until independent evidence is available.
---

NCAAF recommendations must remain Neutral with zero units until both teams satisfy the model's independent current-season evidence requirement. Schedules, logos, sportsbook prices, and forecast snapshots should still flow normally.

**Why:** Opening-week records and team-stat samples can be empty, leaving the generic fallback driven mostly by home advantage and deterministic noise. That can manufacture a large apparent edge with no team-specific basis. Market odds may be displayed but cannot stand in for an independent prediction.

**How to apply:** Keep the conservative gate in every live, scheduled, and replay path. Replace or relax it only after point-in-time preseason priors or another independent early-season model have been walk-forward validated, and preserve the evidence/no-bet reason in immutable snapshots.
