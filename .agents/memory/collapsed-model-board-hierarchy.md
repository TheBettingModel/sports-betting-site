---
name: Collapsed Model Board hierarchy
description: Approved information and visual hierarchy for compact mobile pick cards.
---

Collapsed Model Board cards should show only the sport/board label, game time, matchup, equal-weight team logos, records with home/away, TBM Pick, current odds, recommendation tier, units, and View Analysis. The pick is the visual hero; the treatment should feel compact, premium, balanced, and quietly confident.

Do not add probability, edge, fair price, confidence, model score, stars, CLV, sportsbook branding, opening odds, market movement, or other analytics to the collapsed card. Those belong behind View Analysis.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved the simplified direction and defined the product philosophy as: the card says what TBM likes; View Analysis explains why TBM likes it. Dense collapsed cards undermine scanability and trust.

**How to apply:** Preserve this hierarchy for future Model Board card work. Use disciplined white/lime/gray contrast, equal logo treatment, restrained lime accents, and compact-but-not-crowded spacing across 320–430px mobile widths.

The Today’s Top Pick card follows the same rule as a slightly elevated featured variant. Its collapsed state keeps matchup, pick, current odds, recommendation, units, and View Analysis; pitchers, score, stars, edge, market context, best line, and insights remain behind analysis.

Neutral forecast rows use one primary numeric signal in the collapsed state: TBM Score with “Higher = stronger model conviction.” Win/cover probabilities and pricing remain unchanged but appear only in expanded analysis.

For the V4 Picks feed, the approved collapsed projection hierarchy is even tighter: equal-weight logos with compact team nicknames, one fully readable moneyline pick, projection/official status, and View Analysis. Projected scores, probabilities, model metrics, and pitcher matchups stay expanded.

Sport tabs are permanent navigation. Keep every active product sport visible and clickable even when its current slate is empty; selecting an inactive sport should show an honest sport-specific empty state rather than hiding the tab.
