name: Projection-first Games hub
description: Approved product direction and information hierarchy for complete daily matchup research.
---

The primary mobile experience is a Games hub for researching every scheduled matchup, not a bet tracker. Complete daily slates stay visible by sport and date. Compact rows prioritize matchup, time/status, scores when applicable, and the projected outcome; detailed score, probability, margin, total, and sport-specific analytics belong on the matchup screen.

Unavailable games remain listed with honest evidence or data-quality explanations. Never fabricate a projection to make a slate look complete.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose a projection-resource product over official-pick, units, best-bet, bankroll, and record framing. Lean-only days should still provide useful research across every game, while strict validation remains intact.

**How to apply:** Keep Games, Live, Chat, and Profile as primary navigation. Keep Record hidden from primary navigation. Preserve subscription fail-closed behavior and server-authoritative fixture status. Put deeper analytics behind a fixture tap.

Sport tabs are permanent navigation. Keep every active product sport visible and clickable even when its current slate is empty; selecting an inactive sport should show an honest sport-specific empty state rather than hiding the tab.
