---
name: Forecast feed boundaries
description: Product decision for where complete forecast-only coverage appears in the Picks feed.
---

Individual sport tabs should show all remaining upcoming games in a clearly labeled forecast-only section after Recommended Plays. The combined All tab must remain curated around actual recommendations and its concise no-edge summary.

**Why:** The user wants complete model context when deliberately exploring a sport, without turning the home feed into a long, less actionable slate.

**How to apply:** Keep Buy and Strong Buy treatment confined to Recommended Plays. Do not add a pooled forecast feed to All unless the user explicitly changes this product direction.
